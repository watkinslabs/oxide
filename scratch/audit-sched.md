Read-only survey. Linux source of truth `/home/nd/oxide/linux-master` (v7.2.0-rc4).
Every row verified by reading both sides; no boots, no code changes.

**Corrections to the standing ledgers** (`scratch/partial-gap-triage.md` A1):
`sched::rseq` **does** have `rseq_cs` decode + IP fixup + signature validation,
reachable on both arches (§7) — that A1 row is stale. `MEMBARRIER_CMD_PRIVATE_EXPEDITED_RSEQ`

protection is absent · `CORRECTNESS` wrong observable behaviour · `PERF` right

## What I could not determine

- **Nothing was measured.** Every "does not work" above is a reachability
  argument from reading the call graph. The ~100 ms timeout floor in particular
  is derived from the scanner cadence (`SCAN_PERIOD_NS` × the ktimers 100 ms
  park), not observed — `debug-wakelat` exists to measure exactly this and no
  boot was run.
- **Whether the 100 ms floor is the standing desktop-slowness cause.** It is the
  strongest candidate given `memory: boot-slowness-root-cause`'s open
  "D-Bus/IPC service-activation waits", but attributing it needs a measurement.
- **`SMP>1` behaviour of any SMP row.** The `set_class` double-enqueue, the
  balancer, wake-affine and `membarrier`'s IPI protocol are all reasoned from
  source; `wait-diff-open-items` W4 records that `SMP>1` is untested.
- **`wait4`'s exact `WUNTRACED`/`WCONTINUED` encoding** (`sig<<8|0x7f` / `0xffff`)
  and its one-shot re-report suppression: the notifier side was read, the
  `061_wait4.rs` / `exit/status.rs` consumer side was not read line by line.
- **Whether `HWCAP_SVE` is advertised on aarch64.** Decides whether the missing
  `sve_context` record is ABSENT-OK or a correctness bug.
- **Whether an exception-table catch-all exists** that would make the unguarded
  signal-frame `write_volatile` recoverable. No `access_ok` and no fixup entry
  covering that write were found, but the exception machinery was not enumerated.
- **Real TSC/CNTFRQ calibration accuracy**, and whether QEMU's exposed CPU
  actually has TSC-deadline (the LVT is written unconditionally).
- **Whether `timer_list`'s 32-slot table ever overflows in practice** — a
  `dropped()` counter exists but no consumer was traced.
- **Test coverage of any of this.** Most affected files carry
  `#![cfg(target_os = "oxide-kernel")]`, so per the repo's own phantom-test rule
  any `#[cfg(test)]` inside them never compiles. The ungated, genuinely-tested
  modules — `signum.rs`, `sigaltstack.rs`, `sigwake.rs`, `exit/{notify,orphan}.rs`,
  `syscall/restart.rs`, `101_ptrace/decide.rs`, `tty/jobctl/decide.rs`,
  `cputime.rs`, `affinity.rs`, `timers/clockid.rs` — are where the fewest gaps
  were found. That correlation is the strongest argument in this document for
  moving decision logic out of gated files.
