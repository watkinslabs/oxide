| OPEN | med | `exit_shm` is entirely absent — zero references in the tree, and no per-task SysV-shm creator list. Named as a remaining gap in matrix rows 60 (`exit`) and 231 (`exit_group`) and still open. Consequence: a segment whose creator exits is never unlinked from any creator list and never becomes eligible for the orphan sweep, so `kernel.shm_rmid_forced` (also absent) cannot exist, and `shm_may_destroy` honours only `SHM_DEST` and not the forced-rmid rule. A segment created and left attached by a process that then exits leaks until explicit `IPC_RMID`. Design note for the next lane: the segment needs a creator back-reference (`Weak<Task>`, the analogue of `shm_creator`) so `shm_destroy` can unlink from the creator's list — keying the list on `cpid` alone is unsafe, since tids are reused and that is the string-key side channel the project bans. | F782. `grep -rn 'exit_shm\|shm_clist\|shm_creator'` over `crates/` returns nothing; `ShmSegment` has `cpid` but no creator list; `shm_may_destroy` equivalent at `sysv_shm.rs:224` tests only `SHM_DEST`. | — |
