# B1976 — filesystem remainder PARTIAL rows

Lane: `B1976-fs-remainder-partial-rows`. Scope: the ten filesystem `PARTIAL` rows a
prior lane (B1970) left with their real blocker finally named — 72, 133, 179, 221,
259, 275, 285, 443, 468, 469.

The pattern B1970 reported repeated here: of the six named blockers, **two were
factually wrong about the reference contract** (468/469's device-node claim, 285's
swapfile claim), one was **understated** (179/443 was called an evidence gap and was
three real defects), and one turned out **larger than stated** (221). Corrections are
recorded as first-class results alongside the implementations.
