| FIXED B1951 | MISSING | high | `UFFDIO_REGISTER` refused `MODE_WP` and `MODE_MINOR`, and `UFFDIO_WRITEPROTECT`/`CONTINUE`/`POISON`/`MOVE` did not exist. Fork now skips the strip for a leaf that is already read-only. | `vmm::address_space::fork::tests::fork_does_not_rewrite_an_already_read_only_parent_leaf`; positive control: dropping the `leaf_writable` term makes it RED | — |
| FIXED B1951 | DEFECT | med | A zapped range (`munmap`, `MADV_DONTNEED`) left non-present poison markers in place, so a later mapping of the same address raised a memory error for contents that no longer existed. | FIXED B1951 | DEFECT | med | `UFFDIO_COPY`/`ZEROPAGE` installed anonymous pages with NO reverse-mapping edge and without admitting them for reclaim, so an rmap walk over the mapping could not find a monitor-installed page at all.
