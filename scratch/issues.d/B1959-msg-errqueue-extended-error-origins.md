| FIXED cd0ea6ab7 | DEFECT | HIGH | Dequeuing an extended error re-published the pending errno from whatever record became the new head, and never cleared it. Only ICMP-origin records may own the pending errno, and popping an ICMP record with no ICMP successor must clear it. A transmit completion could therefore hand `SO_ERROR` a zero, and a consumed ICMP error stayed readable forever. | `net::socket_error::tests::queue::{dequeue_clears_pending_errno_when_no_icmp_record_follows, dequeue_promotes_the_next_icmp_record_to_pending_errno, dequeuing_a_transmit_completion_leaves_the_pending_errno_alone}`. Positive control: restoring the old `if let Some(next) = front { errno = next.errno }` reddens the first test. | B1959 |
| FIXED cd0ea6ab7 | DEFECT | MED | Disabling `IP_RECVERR` purged only IPv4 ICMP records and then rewrote the pending errno from the queue.
