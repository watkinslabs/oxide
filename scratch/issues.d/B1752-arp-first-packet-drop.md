# B1752 — first packet to a new neighbour

| Status | Class | Sev | Issue | Evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FIXED B1752 | DEFECT | med | A packet parked on an unresolved neighbour was re-entered into the dispatcher with **no link-layer destination** — the thing it had been waiting for. The reference fills the header from the neighbour's `ha` before releasing the queue; ours left the driver to resolve the hop again through its own state. The first packet to every new neighbour was lost while every later one succeeded: `ping -c3` reported 2 received, and a resolver paid a full timeout on its first query. | `crates/kernel/net/src/tests_arp_next_hop.rs`; positive control — dropping the attach fails 2 tests | — |
| OPEN | DEFECT | high | `drv-virtio-net` keeps its **own** neighbour cache (`modern/neighbor.rs` `resolve_next_hop_mac_observed`), consulted whenever a transmit arrives without an explicit L2 destination. That is a second source of truth for neighbour state beside `net::arp`, with its own entries, its own misses and its own ARP emission — the two can disagree, and a resolution recorded in one is invisible to the other. The reference resolves in the neighbour layer and hands the driver a finished frame. | `grep -n resolve_next_hop_mac_observed crates/drivers/drv-virtio-net/src/modern/netdev.rs` | — |
| OPEN | COVERAGE | low | `ArpCache::insert_at` and `learn` discard the pending jobs `learn_at` returns, so any future caller silently drops every queued packet. Only tests call them today. | `crates/kernel/net/src/arp.rs` | — |
