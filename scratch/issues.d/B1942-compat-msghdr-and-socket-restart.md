## Claims checked and found STALE (no work needed)

- **Row 307, "per-entry `MSG_BATCH` and partial-send stop rules"** — already implemented AND tested:
  `socket::batch::entry_flags` gives every non-final entry `MSG_BATCH` and the final one the caller's
  own flags, and `send_batch` stops after an incomplete send (`outcome.complete`). Covered by
  `socket::tests::sendmmsg_marks_every_nonfinal_entry_as_batched` and
  `socket::tests::phased_batch_returns_completed_prefix_without_failed_copyout`.
- **Rows 299/307, "restart-block/SA_RESTART" as if nothing existed** — the socket side was largely
  built by B1449: `net::sock_intr` returns ERESTARTSYS for an untimed wait and EINTR for one with
  `SO_{RCV,SND}TIMEO`, and `syscalls::net_errno::errno_from_neterr` passes the sentinel through raw.
  `socket::tests::sendmmsg_{returns_a_restart_error_before_any_completed_entry,
  keeps_the_completed_prefix_when_a_later_entry_restarts}` already pinned the send side. What was
  genuinely missing was the RECEIVE batch's coupling, closed above.
- **"restart-block" for sockets** — Linux keeps NO restart block for any socket send/receive; an
  interrupted call restarts whole. Adding one here would be the deviation. Pinned by
  `mmsg_batch::tests::only_the_sentinel_a_batch_returns_can_be_resumed_by_the_tail`.
