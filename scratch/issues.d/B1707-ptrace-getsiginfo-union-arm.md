# B1707 — PTRACE_GETSIGINFO union arm and si_addr

| Status | Sev | Issue | Evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FIXED B1707 | high | `PTRACE_GETSIGINFO` reported every fault stop as a kill record and filled `si_addr` with the TRACER's pid (filed by B1706). Two causes, both fixed: (1) `101_ptrace/sig.rs::getsiginfo` wrote only the `_kill` arm (`signo@0`, `code@8`, `pid@16`, `uid@20`, `value@24`) and never consulted the record's `_sigfault`/`_sigsys`/`_sigpoll` arm, so `si_addr` — bytes 16..24 in the fault arm — was never written; (2) `101_ptrace/stop.rs::notify_with` substituted the tracer's pid into `info.pid` whenever it was zero, unconditionally, so on a SIGSEGV record that word landed in `si_addr`. A debugger could not tell a wild pointer from `kill(2)`. `getsiginfo` now renders through the one `siginfo_t` writer; `notify_with` publishes the record exactly as its producer built it, and the SYNTHESISED event records name the TRACEE (`task_pid_vnr(current)`, `current_uid()`) as the reference kernel does. | Hosted: `hal::siginfo::tests` (18, was 8) + `s101_ptrace_event::tests` (18, was 14). In-guest x86_64: `t_ptrace_siginfo` prints `signo_is_segv=1 code_is_fault=1 addr_matches_fault=1 addr_is_not_a_pid=1`, matching the host oracle. Positive control: deleting the `_sigfault` arm from `write_siginfo` (the literal `_kill`-only write) turns 6 tests RED, one reporting `si_addr` = `0x99_00001234` — a pid and uid packed into the address field, the exact defect shape; deleting the reader's fault arm turns 3 RED. Both restored GREEN. | B1707 |
| FIXED B1707 | high | `PTRACE_SETSIGINFO` and every other `siginfo_t` copy-IN (`rt_sigqueueinfo`, `rt_tgsigqueueinfo`, `pidfd_send_signal`) decoded only the `_kill` arm, so a tracer's GETSIGINFO→SETSIGINFO round trip on a fault stop DESTROYED `si_addr` and re-read it as a sender's pid. The reference kernel's `kernel_siginfo_t` is the union itself, so its copy-in preserves the arm for free; ours is decomposed, so the arm is now recovered from `(si_signo, si_code)` by the same classifier the write side uses. | `hal::read_siginfo`; `every_arm_survives_a_flat_round_trip`, `the_caller_supplied_signal_selects_the_arm_not_the_buffers_si_signo`. | B1707 |

The two hand-rolled ptrace writers and the ptrace reader are deleted; `fs/signalfd` now consumes the classifier instead of carrying its own. One owner: `hal::siginfo` — `layout` (classify), `write_siginfo` (producer-tagged arm), `read_siginfo` (flat decode). | `crates/arch/hal/src/siginfo.rs`; `signalfd` tests unchanged and still green against the moved classifier. | B1707 |
