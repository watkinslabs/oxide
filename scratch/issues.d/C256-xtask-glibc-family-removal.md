| FIXED C256 | HIGH | **The N22 conformance channel was measuring our libc, not our kernel.** `xtask glibc-test` called `sysroot::build_sysroot`, which built `crates/user/{glibc,ldso,folded-stub,crt1}` into `target/sysroot/<triple>/lib`, then linked each guest binary `-nostdlib -Wl,--dynamic-linker=/opt/oxide-conformance/lib/ld-linux-* -l:libc.so.6` and shipped that libc into the image.
