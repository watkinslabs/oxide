| FIXED B1689 | med | `fs` integration tests `nonblock_pipe_buf_vector_is_all_or_nothing` and `large_nonblock_vector_reports_cross_iovec_partial` failed on main. Both encoded the pre-`c1f9f3ab2` conflation of pipe CAPACITY with `PIPE_BUF`; their arithmetic only held while the ring was a fixed 4096 bytes. Production atomicity check was correct throughout. | `cargo test -p fs --test pipe_write_iter --features net/hosted` → 2 failed: `Ok(4097)` vs `Ok(4096)` at `tests/pipe_write_iter.rs:68`, and `Ok(4)` vs `Err(Eagain)` at `:28`. A non-blocking 4097-byte write into an EMPTY 64 KiB pipe writes all 4097 — 4097 > `PIPE_BUF` so no atomicity applies. Rewritten to derive fill amounts from `PipeData::capacity()`; now 4 passed / 0 failed. | B1689 |

| FIXED B1689 | low | `tests/pipe_write_iter.rs` declared its own `const PIPE_BUF: usize = 4096;` — a second source of truth for a value `pipe::limits` owns, so a change to the constant could not reach the test. | Now `use fs::pipe::limits::PIPE_BUF;`. | B1689 |
