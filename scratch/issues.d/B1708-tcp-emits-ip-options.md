# B1708 — TCP emits sticky IP_OPTIONS

| Status | Sev | Issue | Evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FIXED B1708 | med | Sticky `IP_OPTIONS` do not reach TCP segments — `send_tcp_ipv4_segment_in` passed a literal `None` while UDP, raw and ping carried the compiled area. The socket's `IpOpts` is now an `Arc` shared onto `TcpEntry.ip_opts` the way `ip_mtu_discover` is, so the area has one home; `TcpTxPolicy::ipv4_options` hands it to the IPv4 transmit path, and a compiled source route retargets the route lookup, the strict-route screen, the path MTU and the wire destination. Closes the curated rows "Sticky `IP_OPTIONS` do not reach TCP segments", "TCP does not emit IP_OPTIONS", and the residual caveat on the STALE "IP_OPTIONS never emitted on transmit" row. | `net::tests_tcp_ip_options_tx` (4 tests). Positive control: restoring the literal `None` at the call site turns `a_tcp_segment_carries_the_sockets_sticky_option_area` and `a_tcp_source_route_retargets_the_wire_destination_only` RED, 2 passed / 2 failed; restored, 1742/0. | — |
| FIXED B1708 | med | The option area was not charged against the MSS, so a connection carrying one would have emitted segments that no longer fit the path MTU. `net::tcp_ext_hdr` is the ungated owner of the rule (subtract the area, then floor at the minimum send MSS — that order, not the reverse); it is applied at active open, at a learned-PMTU reduction, and at `NetStack::tcp_sync_mss`, which `IP_OPTIONS` set-time now calls for an established connection so a late `setsockopt` cannot leave a stale MSS. | `net::tcp_ext_hdr::tests` (4), `tests_tcp_ip_options_tx::the_sticky_option_area_is_charged_against_the_connections_mss`. Positive control: making `ext_hdr_len` return 0 turns 2 tests RED; restored GREEN. | — |
| OPEN | low | A passive TCP child still carries no IPv4 option area. This is deliberate and matches the reference, which gives an accepted child the *incoming SYN's reversed* option area, not the listener's sticky one — we implement neither, so a child's area is empty either way. Inheriting the listener's sticky area would be a divergence, not a fix. The missing piece is receive-side option reversal (record-route/timestamp echo), which the curated `IP_RETOPTS` row already tracks. | `stack/tcp_listener_deliver.rs` builds children through `TcpEntry::new_bound_full`, whose area is empty by construction. | — |
