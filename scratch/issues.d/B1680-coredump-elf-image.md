| OPEN | high | `coredump::current::write_for_current` still hands the builder an empty mapping list and a zero-filled register block, so a dump written today has no `PT_LOAD` bytes and no registers. | OPEN | med | Nothing selects which mappings are dumped: `CoreSegment::dump_size` is supplied by the caller and the builder honours it verbatim. Linux decides per mapping from `/proc/<pid>/coredump_filter` (anonymous private/shared, file-backed private/shared, ELF headers, hugetlb, DAX), and the ELF-headers bit is why a `dump_size` smaller than the mapping exists. Without the filter every caller has to invent the policy. | `crates/kernel/fs/src/coredump/elf/input.rs`; no `coredump_filter` anywhere in the tree. | dump-filter lane |
| OPEN | med | No `NT_X86_XSTATE` is produced for x86-64 even though the note is plumbed: `CoreThread::xstate` is emitted verbatim when supplied and nothing supplies it. | OPEN | low | `NT_FILE` carries no device or inode number — the format has room only for start, end and page offset per mapping, then the path. This matches the reference format and is not a defect in the builder, recorded so a later lane does not "fix" it by inventing fields. | `files()` in `crates/kernel/fs/src/coredump/elf/notes.rs`; layout asserted in `tests/files.rs`. | — |
