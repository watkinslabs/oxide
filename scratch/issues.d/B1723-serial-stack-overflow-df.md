# B1723 — the `#DF` root-caused: device IRQs have no stack of their own

> **Superseded by B1730 (`4905b1ebd`).** The prescription below — shrink the frames on the
> dispatcher/softirq path — was aimed at the wrong cause. The interrupt stack was not
> overflowing because its frames were too large; it was being run to ZERO headroom by
> interrupts nesting on it without bound, from two x86-only divergences: EOI was sent
> BEFORE the handler (the reference sends it after, so a still-asserted source cannot
> re-enter), and the bottom-half count was taken AFTER `sti`, leaving a window in which
> each nested dispatch opened its own. A static depth walk measures ONE pass, which is
> why it reported ~8.7 KB of 16 KB and passed while the stack died. Frame de-bloat bought
> real headroom and changed nothing about the fault.
